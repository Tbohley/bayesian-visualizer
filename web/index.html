Bayesian Visualizer

A visual workspace for probabilistic programming

Construct, validate, and analyze probabilistic models in an interactive graph editor.

Build a model from the canvas or begin with one of the included presets. Compilation checks the graph for incomplete parameters and structural errors. Once the model is valid, run inference with the sampling settings appropriate to your analysis.

Construction
  • Click anywhere on the canvas to add a node. Select the node to edit its properties in the sidebar.
  • Choose the type of node to place from the left sidebar. Nodes may represent scalar values, deterministic calculations, or random distributions.
  • Hold Shift and select two nodes to connect them. The resulting link can be assigned to a parameter of the receiving node.
  • Click and drag across the canvas to create a plate. New plates contain ten indices by default and begin without an associated dataset.
  • Select a plate, or its square label in the lower-left corner, to configure its extent and associate a dataset.
  • Map random nodes to dataset columns from the plate sidebar to define observation sites. During inference, these observations condition the model on the supplied data.
Analysis
  • Once warmup is complete, posterior samples appear in node histograms as they are generated. You may stop a run early when the model has converged sufficiently for your analysis.
  • Drag across a histogram to brush a subset of samples. The selection remains active until it is cleared from the left sidebar.
  • Open another node histogram to locate the selected samples in a different marginal distribution. Additional brushes are added to the current selection.
  • With one histogram open, hold Shift and select a second node to inspect their joint distribution. Draw a closed region in the joint view to refine the active sample selection.

For a quick introduction, load a preset, compile the graph, and run inference. The histogram of an unobserved variable will open automatically when posterior sampling begins.
